MTA Public Bathrooms

The MTA maintains 125 public bathrooms across 65 different subway stations in New York City. These facilities are generally open from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., with a daily cleaning closure from 12:00 p.m. to 1:00 p.m.. Locations are spread across the boroughs, including key hubs, and are often listed on the official MTA Bathroom webpage.

Food Insecurity in New York City

Over 1.2 million experience food insecurity each year, meaning they do not always have reliable access to enough affordable, nutritious food. The New York City Council and organizations like Food Bank For New York City report that demand for food assistance has remained significantly higher than pre-pandemic levels, with many families relying on food pantries regularly.

Homelessness & Shelter in NYC

Over 80,000 people people stay in the New York City shelter system on an average night, including thousands of families with children. According to City the New York Department of Homeless Services, New York City operates the largest municipal shelter system in the United States, providing temporary housing, meals, and support services.
